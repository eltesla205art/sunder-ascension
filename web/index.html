THE MISSION

Before Egypt, there was Atlantis. Now the Crystal that drowned it has returned and raised the Nine Bows — every enemy Egypt ever faced, reborn in crystal and fury. Pick one of three starcraft and fight through 9 escalating stages, each with its own faction and boss, to face THE CRYSTAL OVERLORD. Collect power-ups, evolve your ship's form, and break all nine.

CONTROLS

🖥 COMPUTER

  • WASD←↑↓→ move
  • SPACE fire / confirm
  • X bomb (clears bullets, hurts bosses)
  • M music on/off
  • ESC back to title

📱 PHONE / TABLET

  • Drag anywhere to fly — your ship autofires
  • Tap the ☥ button to bomb
  • Tap left / right side on ship select to browse
  • Tap to advance story screens
  • Tip: turn your sound on 🔊
