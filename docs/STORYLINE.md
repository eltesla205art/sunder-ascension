# ATLANTEAN BLUEPRINT: THE NINE BOWS — Storyline

## The Premise
Before Egypt, there was **Atlantis**. When it fell, its last engineers hid their greatest weapon beneath the sands — **the Sunborn Thunder**, a starcraft grown from a single seed of gold. It slept for ten thousand years.

Now the sky burns. **The Crystal** — the ancient enemy that drowned Atlantis — has returned. It has raised **the Nine Bows**: every foe Egypt ever faced, reborn in crystal and fury. You are **ELTESLA**, heir of the old blood. The Thunder answers only to you.

*(ELTESLA ties directly to your ELTesla205 Soul identity — the pilot is you.)*

## The Nine Bows — the campaign arc
Egypt's real historical "Nine Bows" were its traditional enemies. Here they're the nine stages, each a step closer to the truth of what drowned Atlantis:

1. **The Nubians** — Archer King Taharqa. The first test. The Crystal watches how you fight.
2. **The Libyans** — Desert Warlord Meshwesh. The desert's old grudge, faster and in force.
3. **The Hyksos** — Chariot Lord Khyan. They conquered Egypt once; now they ride chariots of light.
4. **The Sea Peoples** — Wave Tyrant Peleset. Homeless drowned nations who'll take yours.
5. **The Hittites** — Iron Charioteer Muwatalli. The iron empire. Halfway — the Crystal grows impatient.
6. **The Mitanni** — Maryannu Rider Tushratta. Horse-lords who circle before they strike.
7. **The Assyrians** — Siege King Ashurbanipal. The siege that never ends; where lesser pilots fall.
8. **The Persians** — Immortal Shah Cambyses. The endless Immortal Host. The gate lies beyond him.
9. **The Nine Bows United** — **THE CRYSTAL OVERLORD.** All nine as one, and behind them the enemy of Atlantis itself. Everything ends here.

## The Ending
Break all nine and the Overlord shatters into falling stars; the sky heals gold. Ten thousand years of waiting, answered — Atlantis avenged, Egypt eternal. The Thunder returns to the sand to sleep again... until the sky next burns.

## How the story plays in-game
- **Opening crawl** on launch (press Space to begin)
- **Stage briefing** before each stage — a quote + a short mission brief
- **Boss taunt card** when each boss appears
- **Stage-clear line** after each boss falls ("One bow broken...")
- **Victory / defeat** endings with full narration
All of it lives in `scripts/StoryData.gd` — edit any line freely.
